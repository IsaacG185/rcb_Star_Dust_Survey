SExtractor : not circular, outline around star (isophotal magnitude), we use photutils
WCSTools : RA and Dec to WCS
IRAF's CCMAP task : fits a more refined coordinate model, called a TNX solution, which also accounts for optical distortion
Reference catalog is Kepler Input Catalog (KIC), GSC, and AAVSO

Solutions to some of the issues: 

Blended stars : FWHM, compare to KIC, calculate if unreliable
Plate defects : distinguishes real stars from junk by comparing several shape-related measurements from SExtractor (blur width, ellipticity, 
position angle [the compass-like direction that oval is tilted], and total light detected) for confirmed real stars versus everything else, then statistically flags outliers as likely defects.
Local brightness correction : we did this in SISaP
