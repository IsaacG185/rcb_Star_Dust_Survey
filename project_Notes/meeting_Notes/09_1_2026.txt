DASCH rejection pipeline:
    - Stage 1 [Scanning: 7,218 → 5,403]
        - Total catalog exposures for V CrA's field vs. those actually digitized (scanned into a usable cutout image). 1,812 exposures exist in the archive but were never scanned;
        3 errored on download. Pure physical-archive coverage, unrelated to photometry.
    - Stage 2 [Photometric pipeline survival: 5,403 → 2,846]
        - Of the scanned plates, only 2,846 ever came out of DASCH's photometric pipeline with a usable calibrated solution — the other ~2,557 have a real scanned image but
        never made it through processing. This is the single largest cut in the funnel, larger than the other three stages combined. Confirmed not to be about catalog choice:
        switching from APASS to ATLAS (~1 billion stars vs. APASS's much sparser catalog) barely changed the count (2,846 → 2,851), ruling out reference-catalog density as
        the cause. Tracing DASCH's actual pipeline source, the bottleneck instead sits in the catalog-independent stages every plate must clear before any refcat is even
        involved — most likely a blind astrometric WCS solve (up to 5 attempted configurations before the pipeline gives up on a plate entirely), with SExtractor detection
        failure and SCAMP crashes as secondary contributors. Best-supported physical causes: shallow/underexposed plates, physical plate degradation (fogging, scratches),
        tracking errors or optical distortion, and star fields too sparse or difficult for the solver's time budget. Not confirmed against V CrA's actual per-plate pipeline
        logs — this is strong code-level inference plus one direct A/B test, not a verified log trace.
    - Stage 3 [Detection: 2,846 → 1,818]
        - Of the plates with a successful photometric solution, 1,818 actually detected V CrA; 1,028 are genuine non-detections (star too faint for that plate's depth that
        night). limiting_mag_local is populated for all rows either way, confirming these are real "looked, not visible" measurements.
    - Stage 4 [DASCH's automatic quality rejection: 1,818 → 1,507]
        Running apply_standard_rejections() flagged 311 detections as low-quality (bad AFLAGS), leaving 1,507 clean points — this is your "~300 later rejected." Not applied 
        automatically; must be called explicitly. DASCH's own warning: still under development, don't rely on it to fully clean data.
